METHOD / LIMITS

How the shortlist is built

1. Daily kill history

Use only LWS Pro daily-history kill readings from 3 August through 2 October. A comparable record needs at least 20 positive kill readings across 45 days, a recent 28-day span of at least 20 days, an observation within five days of 2 October, and at least six observable weeks. The current snapshot's kill total is not used in the fit score.

2. Participation

Full-period and recent kill pace are gained kills divided by observed days. An active week has a positive gain between its first and last observed readings across at least four days. Cumulative kills must not decrease. “Core” means both kill paces exceed the medians of players with comparable history, at least six weeks are active, and active weeks represent at least 75% of observed weeks.

3. Power-first ranking

The fit score combines percentile standing in THP across all current 1616 players (60%), full-period kill pace among comparable players (15%), recent kill pace (10%), and active-week share (15%). The weights are a planning choice, not a game formula. Players without comparable kill history have no fit score and stay visible for manual review.
