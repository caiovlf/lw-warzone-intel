Reading this comparison

Combined squad power is the arithmetic sum of Tank, Missile and Air power. The percentage beside it is the difference divided by Buzaluga's total. Individual squad gaps use the lower power as the percentage baseline. These values describe the supplied snapshot and do not predict the duel outcome.

Missing values stay missing: N/A is not treated as zero. Slash-separated chip levels, weapon progression and Overlord skills are preserved without assigning meanings to their individual positions. “All red” is kept as a label because the workbook does not specify its item count. The asterisk in Overlord skills is retained from the source.
