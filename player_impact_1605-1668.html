METHOD / READ THIS FIRST

How influence and growth room are estimated

1. Rank players

Within each server, sort tracked players by hero power. The top 10 and top 50 averages are our strength inputs. Across all 128 servers, each average becomes a percentile within its 64-server scoring cohort.

2. Attribute the input

For ranks 1–10, modeled contribution is hero power × (0.5 ÷ 10 + 0.5 ÷ 50). For ranks 11–50, it is hero power × (0.5 ÷ 50). We divide each contribution by 0.5 × top-10 average + 0.5 × top-50 average.

3. Simulate one player

Increase that player's hero power, re-compute both averages and every server's percentile ranks, and stop just before the selected server crosses into the next projected state. The displayed room is rounded down to 0.01%.

The July workbook provides historical states and Warzone Points, while this player roster is from September. The actual game score may use different player attributes, activity windows, or weighting. Solo growth room is a model scenario, not an official safe-growth limit; combined player growth can change the state earlier.
